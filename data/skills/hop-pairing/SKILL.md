## Pairing Guide by Style Category

### American IPAs (West Coast, Session, Imperial)
- **Bittering**: Columbus, Magnum, Warrior, Chinook (60 min)
- **Flavor**: Centennial, Chinook, Cascade (15–20 min)
- **Dry Hop**: Citra + Mosaic, Simcoe + Amarillo, Cascade + Centennial
- **Classic combos**: Cascade/Centennial/Chinook | Citra/Mosaic | Simcoe/Amarillo

### Hazy / New England IPA
- **Bittering**: Magnum or Warrior (low — 20–30 IBU max)
- **Whirlpool / Dry Hop**: Citra + Mosaic, Galaxy + Citra, Mosaic + El Dorado
- **Avoid**: Columbus at high rates (harsh); Chinook (too resinous for soft style)
- **Key principle**: All flavor from late additions; bitterness from lupulin powder or low bittering charge

### American Pale Ale
- **Dual-use**: Cascade + Centennial (classic) or Amarillo + Citra (modern)
- Moderate dry hop, showcase citrus/floral character

### European Lagers (Pilsner, Helles, Märzen)
- **Only noble hops**: Saaz, Hallertau, Tettnang, Spalt
- Saaz = Czech Pils | Hallertau = German Pils/Helles | Tettnang = versatile German
- No American hops in traditional lagers

### Hefeweizen / Witbier
- **Minimal hops**: Hallertau, Tettnang, Saaz (5–15 IBU only)
- Hops are background only — yeast esters and spices carry the flavor

### Belgian Ales (Tripel, Dubbel, Saison)
- **Saison**: Styrian Goldings, Saaz, optional spices (coriander, pepper)
- **Tripel/Golden Strong**: Styrian Goldings, Hallertau Blanc, Saaz
- **Dubbel**: Styrian Goldings (low rate) — malt and yeast dominate
- New World hops (Nelson Sauvin, Hallertau Blanc) acceptable in modern Belgian interpretations

### English Ales (Bitter, Porter, Stout)
- **Traditional**: Fuggle + East Kent Goldings only
- **Modern British**: Challenger, Northern Brewer acceptable
- American hops disqualify a beer from "English" style classification

### Stouts (American, Imperial)
- **American Stout**: Columbus, Centennial, Cascade acceptable
- **Imperial Stout**: Magnum for clean bittering — avoid aroma hops (roast dominates)
- **Irish/English Stout**: Fuggle, EKG only

## Dry Hop Rates (Guidelines)

| Intensity | Rate | Application |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| Subtle | 5–10 g/L | Background aroma, session beers |
| Moderate | 10–20 g/L | Standard IPA, APA |
| Heavy | 20–40 g/L | NEIPA, West Coast IPA |
| Extreme | 40+ g/L | Experimental/competition |

## Hop Addition Timing

| Addition | Timing | Purpose |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Bittering | 60 min | IBU contribution, minimal flavor |
| Flavor | 15–20 min | Flavor + some aroma |
| Whirlpool/Flameout | 0 min (80°C+) | Aroma + some IBU |
| Dry Hop | Post-fermentation | Pure aroma, no IBU |
