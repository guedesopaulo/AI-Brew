# BJCP Style Reference

Format: Name | OG | IBU | SRM | ABV% | Key Malts | Key Hops | Yeast Notes

## Lagers

### American Light Lager
OG 1.028–1.040 | IBU 8–12 | SRM 2–3 | ABV 2.8–4.2%
Base: 2-row + 20–40% corn/rice adjunct | No specialty malts
Hops: Cluster, Saaz (minimal) | Yeast: Clean lager (W-34/70), very high attenuation
Notes: High carbonation, very low flavor. Avoid over-hopping.

### American Lager
OG 1.040–1.050 | IBU 8–18 | SRM 2–4 | ABV 4.2–5.3%
Base: 2-row + up to 30% adjunct
Hops: Cluster, Saaz (minimal late) | Yeast: Clean lager, 80%+ attenuation
Notes: Slightly more body than light lager. Clean fermentation is critical.

### German Pilsner
OG 1.044–1.050 | IBU 25–45 | SRM 2–5 | ABV 4.4–5.2%
Base: Pilsner malt (100%)
Hops: Hallertau, Saaz, Tettnang (late aroma) | Yeast: Bohemian/Bavarian lager
Notes: Pronounced noble hop bitterness. Very pale, dry finish.

### Bohemian Pilsner
OG 1.044–1.056 | IBU 30–45 | SRM 3–6 | ABV 4.2–5.4%
Base: Pilsner malt (100%), soft water
Hops: Saaz (only) | Yeast: Bohemian lager (WLP800/2124)
Notes: Softer, rounder maltiness than German Pils. Spicy Saaz aroma.

### Munich Helles
OG 1.044–1.048 | IBU 16–22 | SRM 3–5 | ABV 4.7–5.4%
Base: Pilsner malt (95%+), small % Munich malt
Hops: Hallertau, Tettnang (very subtle) | Yeast: Bavarian lager
Notes: Malt-forward, gentle balance. No hop aroma to speak of.

### Märzen / Oktoberfest
OG 1.054–1.060 | IBU 18–24 | SRM 8–17 | ABV 5.8–6.3%
Base: Munich malt (50%), Vienna malt (30%), Pilsner (20%)
Hops: Hallertau, Tettnang (low) | Yeast: Bavarian lager
Notes: Rich toasty/bread malt. Clean lager fermentation. No fruitiness.

### Munich Dunkel
OG 1.048–1.056 | IBU 18–28 | SRM 17–28 | ABV 4.5–5.6%
Base: Dark Munich malt (70–80%), Pilsner malt
Hops: Hallertau, Spalt (low) | Yeast: Bavarian lager
Notes: Chocolate, bread crust, mild coffee. No roast astringency.

### Schwarzbier
OG 1.046–1.052 | IBU 20–35 | SRM 25–30 | ABV 4.4–5.4%
Base: Pilsner (50%), Munich (20%), Carafa III (15%), Chocolate malt (10%)
Hops: Perle, Hallertau (moderate) | Yeast: Bavarian lager
Notes: Dark but light-bodied. Roast without stout heaviness.

### German Bock
OG 1.064–1.072 | IBU 20–27 | SRM 14–22 | ABV 6.3–7.2%
Base: Munich malt (70%), Vienna malt (30%)
Hops: Hallertau, Tettnang (very low) | Yeast: Bavarian lager
Notes: Melanoidin-rich, warming, low bitterness. Malt is the star.

## American Ales

### American Pale Ale
OG 1.045–1.060 | IBU 30–50 | SRM 5–10 | ABV 4.5–6.2%
Base: 2-row pale (90%+) | Specialty: Crystal 20–40L (5–10%)
Hops: Cascade, Centennial, Amarillo (late) | Yeast: US-05 or WLP001
Notes: Citrus/floral hop aroma. Clean malt base. Moderate bitterness.

### American IPA
OG 1.056–1.070 | IBU 40–70 | SRM 6–14 | ABV 5.5–7.5%
Base: 2-row pale (90%+) | Specialty: Crystal 40L (≤10%)
Hops: Cascade/Centennial/Chinook/Citra/Simcoe (bittering + heavy late/dry hop)
Yeast: US-05 or WLP001 (clean, 75–80% attenuation)
Notes: Resinous, citrusy, or tropical. Firm bitterness. Dry finish.

### West Coast IPA
OG 1.060–1.070 | IBU 50–80 | SRM 6–10 | ABV 6.0–7.5%
Base: 2-row (95%), minimal crystal
Hops: Simcoe, Columbus, Centennial (high bittering + dry hop)
Yeast: WLP001 or US-05 (very high attenuation)
Notes: Crystal clear, very dry, piney/resinous. High perceived bitterness.

### Hazy / New England IPA
OG 1.060–1.080 | IBU 25–60 | SRM 3–7 | ABV 6.0–9.0%
Base: 2-row (60%), Flaked oats (20%), Wheat (20%)
Hops: Citra/Mosaic/Galaxy/Amarillo (massive dry hop, low bittering)
Yeast: WLP066 London Ale III or Wyeast 1318 (juicy, fruity, full body)
Notes: Permanent haze from proteins. Soft, juicy, low bitterness. No dry finish.

### Double / Imperial IPA
OG 1.065–1.100 | IBU 60–120 | SRM 6–14 | ABV 7.5–10.0%
Base: 2-row (85%+), small % sugar to dry out
Hops: Columbus/Simcoe/Citra (heavy bittering + dry hop)
Yeast: US-05 at high pitch rate
Notes: Huge hop and malt. High ABV needs balancing malt backbone.

### Session IPA
OG 1.040–1.052 | IBU 30–50 | SRM 4–7 | ABV 3.0–5.0%
Base: 2-row, minimal crystal | Keep grain bill light
Hops: Citra/Mosaic/Amarillo (late + dry hop for aroma, not bitterness)
Yeast: US-05 (clean)
Notes: All the aroma, fraction of the ABV. Easy to over-bitter at low gravity.

## English Ales

### English Bitter / ESB
OG 1.048–1.060 | IBU 25–40 | SRM 8–14 | ABV 4.6–6.2%
Base: Maris Otter (80%), Crystal 60L (10%), Biscuit malt (5%)
Hops: Fuggle, East Kent Goldings (bittering + late) | Yeast: WLP002 or 1968
Notes: Biscuity, earthy, slightly fruity. Moderate carbonation. British character.

## Amber / Dark Ales

### American Amber Ale
OG 1.045–1.060 | IBU 25–40 | SRM 11–17 | ABV 4.5–6.2%
Base: 2-row (75%), Crystal 60L (10%), Crystal 120L (5%)
Hops: Cascade, Centennial (moderate) | Yeast: US-05
Notes: Toasty caramel malt + American hop character. Balanced.

### American Brown Ale
OG 1.045–1.060 | IBU 20–30 | SRM 18–35 | ABV 4.3–6.2%
Base: 2-row (70%), Crystal 60L (10%), Chocolate malt (5%), Brown malt (5%)
Hops: Willamette, Cascade (moderate) | Yeast: US-05
Notes: Chocolate, nutty, moderate hop. More hop-forward than English Brown.

## Porters & Stouts

### English Porter
OG 1.040–1.052 | IBU 18–35 | SRM 20–30 | ABV 4.0–5.4%
Base: Pale malt (70%), Brown malt (15%), Chocolate malt (10%)
Hops: Fuggle, Challenger (moderate) | Yeast: WLP002 or US-05
Notes: Chocolate, light coffee, moderate body. Less roasty than stout.

### Robust Porter
OG 1.048–1.065 | IBU 25–40 | SRM 22–35 | ABV 4.8–6.5%
Base: 2-row (65%), Chocolate malt (12%), Crystal 60L (8%), Black malt (5%)
Hops: Northern Brewer, Centennial (moderate) | Yeast: US-05
Notes: Bolder roast than English Porter. More body, more bitterness.

### Irish Dry Stout
OG 1.036–1.044 | IBU 30–45 | SRM 25–40 | ABV 4.0–5.0%
Base: Pale malt (70%), Flaked barley (15%), Roasted barley (10%)
Hops: Fuggle, East Kent Goldings (moderate) | Yeast: WLP004 Irish Ale
Notes: Dry, roasty finish. Flaked barley for creamy head. Low gravity, big flavor.

### American Stout
OG 1.050–1.075 | IBU 35–75 | SRM 30–40 | ABV 5.0–7.0%
Base: 2-row (75%), Chocolate malt (10%), Roasted barley (8%)
Hops: Columbus, Centennial (moderate to high) | Yeast: US-05
Notes: More hop-forward than Irish Stout. American hop character acceptable.

### Imperial Stout
OG 1.075–1.115 | IBU 50–90 | SRM 30–40 | ABV 8.0–12.0%
Base: 2-row (60%), Chocolate malt (12%), Roasted barley (10%), Crystal 120L (8%)
Hops: Columbus, Magnum (high bittering, minimal aroma)
Yeast: US-05 at very high pitch rate, may need nutrient additions
Notes: Huge, complex, warming. IBU needed to balance extreme malt sweetness.

### Oatmeal Stout
OG 1.048–1.065 | IBU 25–40 | SRM 22–40 | ABV 4.2–5.9%
Base: Pale malt (65%), Flaked oats (15%), Chocolate malt (10%), Roasted barley (5%)
Hops: Fuggle, East Kent Goldings (moderate) | Yeast: WLP002 or US-05
Notes: Silky mouthfeel from oats. Smoother than regular stout.

## Wheat Beers

### Hefeweizen
OG 1.044–1.052 | IBU 8–15 | SRM 3–9 | ABV 4.9–5.6%
Base: Wheat malt (50–60%), Pilsner malt (40–50%)
Hops: Hallertau, Tettnang (very low) | Yeast: Wyeast 3068 (banana/clove)
Notes: Banana ester + clove phenol. Ferment cool for clove, warm for banana.

### Witbier / Belgian Witbier
OG 1.044–1.052 | IBU 10–20 | SRM 2–4 | ABV 4.5–5.5%
Base: Pilsner malt (50%), Unmalted wheat (40%), Flaked oats (10%)
Hops: Saaz (low) + coriander + orange peel (spice additions)
Yeast: Wyeast 3944 (fruity, spicy, tart)
Notes: Hazy, spiced, refreshing. Unmalted wheat is critical for haze and body.

### American Wheat
OG 1.040–1.055 | IBU 15–30 | SRM 3–6 | ABV 4.0–5.5%
Base: 2-row (50%), Wheat malt (50%)
Hops: Cascade, Centennial (moderate) | Yeast: US-05 (clean)
Notes: Clean fermentation (unlike Hefeweizen). American hop character OK.

## Belgian Ales

### Belgian Tripel
OG 1.075–1.085 | IBU 20–40 | SRM 4–7 | ABV 7.5–9.5%
Base: Pilsner malt (85%), Candi sugar clear (15%)
Hops: Styrian Goldings, Hallertau Blanc (moderate) | Yeast: Wyeast 3787
Notes: Fruity, spicy, warming. High ABV must be well-hidden.

### Belgian Dubbel
OG 1.062–1.075 | IBU 15–25 | SRM 10–17 | ABV 6.0–7.6%
Base: Pilsner malt (70%), Dark candi sugar (15%), Special B (10%)
Hops: Styrian Goldings, Hallertau (low) | Yeast: Wyeast 1762 Abbey II
Notes: Raisin, dark fruit, malty. Low hop bitterness. Complex yeast esters.

### Belgian Golden Strong Ale
OG 1.070–1.095 | IBU 22–35 | SRM 3–6 | ABV 7.5–10.5%
Base: Pilsner malt (80%), Candi sugar clear (20%)
Hops: Styrian Goldings, Saaz (moderate) | Yeast: Wyeast 1388 Belgian Strong
Notes: Deceptively pale and light. Fruity, spicy, dangerously drinkable.

### Saison / Farmhouse Ale
OG 1.048–1.065 | IBU 20–35 | SRM 5–14 | ABV 5.0–7.0%
Base: Pilsner malt (80%), Wheat (10%), Spelt or Rye (5–10%)
Hops: Saaz, Styrian Goldings + optional spices
Yeast: WLP565 or Wyeast 3724 (fruity, peppery, highly attenuating — ferment warm)
Notes: Dry, effervescent, complex. High final attenuation is critical.

## Other Styles

### Kölsch
OG 1.044–1.050 | IBU 18–30 | SRM 3–5 | ABV 4.4–5.2%
Base: Pilsner malt (90%), Wheat malt (10%)
Hops: Hallertau, Tettnang, Spalt (low–moderate) | Yeast: Wyeast 2565 Kölsch
Notes: Delicate, clean ale with lager-like character. Ferment cold (13–16°C).

### California Common
OG 1.048–1.054 | IBU 30–45 | SRM 10–14 | ABV 4.5–5.5%
Base: 2-row (90%), Crystal 40L (10%)
Hops: Northern Brewer (only — historical style) | Yeast: Wyeast 2112 California Lager
Notes: Ferments warm (18°C) with lager yeast. Toasty, woody Northern Brewer character.

### Scottish Ale / Wee Heavy
OG 1.070–1.130 | IBU 17–35 | SRM 14–25 | ABV 6.5–10.0%
Base: Maris Otter (85%), Crystal 80L (10%)
Hops: East Kent Goldings (low — malt-focused) | Yeast: WLP028 Edinburgh
Notes: Very malt-forward, caramel, toffee. Low hop bitterness. Kettle caramelization adds color.

### Altbier
OG 1.044–1.052 | IBU 25–48 | SRM 11–17 | ABV 4.3–5.5%
Base: Munich malt (70%), Pilsner malt (20%), Carafa I (5%)
Hops: Spalt, Hallertau (moderate) | Yeast: WLP036 Düsseldorf Alt or Wyeast 1007
Notes: Hop-forward for a malt-driven ale. Ferment cool (15–18°C) for clean character.

### English Mild
OG 1.030–1.038 | IBU 10–25 | SRM 12–25 | ABV 3.0–3.8%
Base: Pale malt (70%), Crystal 60L (15%), Brown malt (10%)
Hops: Fuggle, EKG (very low) | Yeast: WLP002 or 1968
Notes: Very low ABV session beer. Malt character must shine despite low gravity.
